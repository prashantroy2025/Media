# Royalty-Free Background Music Directory

Place your `.mp3` or `.wav` royalty-free background music tracks in this directory.

## Safety & Copyright Rules
1. **Zero Copyright Infringement:** Never use copyrighted commercial songs or TikTok/Instagram trending audio clips. Automated content detection will copyright-strike or demonetize your videos.
2. **Safe Sources for Free Music:**
   - **YouTube Audio Library:** Download tracks directly from your YouTube Studio dashboard (Filter by "Attribution not required", Genre: Hip Hop, Dance & Electronic, Cinematic).
   - **Pixabay Music:** [pixabay.com/music/](https://pixabay.com/music/) (Royalty-free for commercial use, no attribution required for most tracks).
   - **Free Music Archive (FMA):** Filter for Public Domain or CC-BY commercial.
   - **Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod):** High-energy background themes with simple CC-BY attribution.

## Recommended Moods
For AI and tech explainers:
- **Upbeat / Tech House:** Energetic synth baselines (120-128 BPM).
- **Lo-Fi Chill Hop:** Subtle rhythmic focus beat (80-90 BPM).
- **Cyberpunk / Synthwave:** Futuristic tech vibe.

## Automatic Audio Ducking
Nova Autopilot automatically lowers the music volume (ducks to ~5-12%) whenever the narration voice is speaking, and fades out cleanly at video completion.
If this folder is empty, the pipeline will render videos with voice-only audio and log a notification.
