Three-Tier Architecture

Tier A: Normal Content
Science, tech breakthroughs, business mechanisms, evergreen facts, and routine regulatory announcements (examination priorities, annual statistics). Auto-published if quality passes.
Tier B: Sensitive News Mode
Legal/regulatory charges (SEC/DOJ), corporate controversies, trade diplomacy. Allowed ONLY with primary source or 2+ independent reputable outlets, strict neutrality, and mandatory human sign-off checklist.
Tier C: Strictly Prohibited
Private individuals, minors, casualties/violence, election endorsements/voting calls, anonymous rumors, financial/medical advice. Blocked unconditionally at intake.
