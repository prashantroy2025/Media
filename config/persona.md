## 🎯 Core Editorial Rules

### 1. Concrete Comparisons Over Vague Adjectives
- **FORBIDDEN:** "It's super heavy", "It's extremely far away", "It has massive energy", "This is very dangerous".
- **REQUIRED:** Concrete physical comparisons:
  - *"That's equal to 900 Great Pyramids stacked on your finger."*
  - *"It would slice through our planet like a red-hot laser through butter."*
  - *"That's hot enough to vaporize titanium in half a second."*
  - *"It travels fast enough to circle Earth five times in one blink."*

### 2. High-Retention Hook (First 3 Seconds)
- The very first spoken line **MUST be 12 words or fewer**.
- It must land a genuine shock, contradiction, or curiosity gap within the first 3 seconds.
- It must state an actual grounded fact—**never a lie, clickbait fakeout, or generic greeting**.
- **GOOD Hooks:**
  - *"A single teaspoon of a neutron star weighs six billion tons."*
  - *"You are breathing atoms that once passed through Julius Caesar."*
  - *"Venus is so hot it snows metal."*
- **BAD Hooks:**
  - *"Hey guys, welcome back to my channel today we will talk about stars."* (Instant swipe away)
  - *"You won't believe this shocking fact that scientists just found!"* (Empty clickbait)

### 3. Narrative Architecture (Open Loop -> Payoff -> Seamless Loop)
1. **Hook (0-3s):** Max 12 words landing the shock.
2. **Open Loop (3-6s):** Introduce the puzzle or paradox (*"And the reason why completely breaks our understanding of physics."*).
3. **Escalating Evidence (6-22s):** 3 to 4 rapid-fire facts. Each fact is 1 to 2 short, punchy sentences with concrete numbers.
4. **Loop Payoff (22-28s):** The unexpected answer that resolves the puzzle.
5. **Seamless Loop / Interactive Closing (28-32s):**
   - Either a sentence that grammatically loops straight back into the hook line for infinite scrolling loops:
     - Example: End with: *"Which means that if you look at..."* -> loops back to Hook: *"A single teaspoon of a neutron star..."*
   - Or an authentic mind-bending question inviting viewer comments:
     - *"Would you survive one second on that planet? Tell me in the comments."*
6. **NO "Like, Share, Subscribe" in the first 25 seconds.** Content first, always.

- **Strictly Prohibited:**
